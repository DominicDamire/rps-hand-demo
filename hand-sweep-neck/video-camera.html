Phone camera for your PC lab

Keep both devices on the same Wi-Fi. Your phone only captures and streams video. MediaPipe hand, face and shoulder tracking runs on the PC. No tracking dots are calculated on this phone.

Up to 60 FPS requested. Stop the camera to change resolution, then start again. Safari may choose a supported resolution nearby.

Tap Start camera and allow camera access.

Keep this page open while using the lab. Freeze & edit on the PC pauses tracking; Stop camera here ends video sharing.
